# 🚀 Portfolio Website Master Plan: The "AI Infrastructure Expert" Strategy

**Author**: Gemini (AI Assistant) & Bill Hsu

---

## Part 1: 🗣️ The "Human" Narrative Guide (Read This First)

**Target Audience**: AI Assistants & Designers working on this portfolio.
**The Goal**: Stop generating generic "LinkedIn Sales Pitch" content. We need to tell the story of a **Human Engineer solving Hard Problems**.

### 1. The Voice: "The Engineer's Voice"
*   **❌ Bad (Salesy)**: "I am a passionate SRE with expertise in Kubernetes driving high-impact projects."
*   **✅ Good (Authentic)**: "At Amazon, I saw 7,000 GPUs sitting idle. That wasn't just money lost; it was science delayed. So, I built 'Leviathan'—an automated reaper to fix them."
*   **Rule**: Focus on the **Struggle**, the **Insight**, and the **Solution**.

### 2. The 3-Act Structure
We are telling three specific stories about Bill's career:
1.  **Act 1: The Cleaner (Amazon)**. Theme: **Efficiency**. Transforming chaos into automated discipline ($9M savings).
2.  **Act 2: The Architect (Alibaba)**. Theme: **Scale**. Connecting isolated clusters to train massive robots (10k GPUs).
3.  **Act 3: The Builder (Personal)**. Theme: **Craftsmanship**. Building polished, serverless products, not just backend scripts.

### 3. Visual UX Strategy
*   **"The Workbench" Aesthetic**: Dark mode, neon accents, monospace fonts for data.
*   **Contextual Metrics**: Don't just list numbers. Show comparisons (e.g., "10 hours manual debugging → 1 hour automated fix").
